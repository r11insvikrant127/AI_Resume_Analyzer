ACCENTURE – INTERVIEW AND RECRUITMENT PROCESS

Overview

Accenture's recruitment process varies depending on the role, location,
experience level, and skills required.

There is no single fixed interview process that applies to every position.

Typical Recruitment Flow

1. Application Submission

The candidate applies for a specific position through the Accenture careers
platform or another authorized recruiting channel.

After applying, the candidate receives confirmation that the application has
been received.

2. Application Review

Recruiters review the candidate's application and assess how the candidate's
skills and experience match the role.

Recruiters may also identify other suitable opportunities, but a separate
application may be required for another role.

3. Interview Invitation

If the candidate is selected to continue, Accenture contacts the candidate to
schedule an interview.

Communication may take place through email or phone.

4. Interview

Interviews may be conducted by phone, video, or in person.

The number and type of interviews depend on the specific role.

Interview topics may include:

- Technical knowledge
- Problem solving
- Role-specific skills
- Previous projects and experience
- Communication
- Behavioral questions
- Situational questions

Candidates should be prepared to explain projects listed on their resume,
including their responsibilities, technical decisions, challenges, and results.

5. Technical Assessment

For some roles, candidates may be asked to complete an online technical
assessment.

The assessment may evaluate:

- Problem solving
- Logical reasoning
- Applied technical knowledge
- Coding ability
- Ability to structure a solution

Accenture states that assessments are designed around skills relevant to the
role.

Assessment Timing

The assessment is generally completed online within the timeframe specified
in the invitation.

Accenture states that assessments are typically completed in one sitting and
may take approximately 30–90 minutes depending on the assessment and
candidate proficiency.

The invitation provides the applicable time limit.

6. Skills Assessment and Role-Specific Evaluation

Depending on the position, candidates may be evaluated on technical skills,
natural strengths, decision-making, or other capabilities relevant to the
role.

The exact assessment depends on the position.

7. Final Decision

After the relevant interviews and assessments, the recruiting team evaluates
the candidate.

If selected, Accenture contacts the candidate regarding an offer and the next
steps.

Important Interview Preparation Areas

Candidates applying for entry-level technology roles should be prepared to
discuss:

Technical Fundamentals:
- Data Structures and Algorithms
- Object-Oriented Programming
- DBMS and SQL
- Operating Systems
- Computer Networks
- Software Engineering

Programming:
- One or more programming languages
- Problem solving
- Coding questions
- Debugging

Projects:
- Project architecture
- Technologies used
- Individual contribution
- Challenges faced
- Technical decisions
- Improvements and future scope

Behavioral Questions:
- Tell me about yourself.
- Why do you want to join Accenture?
- Why should we hire you?
- What are your strengths and weaknesses?
- Describe a challenge you faced.
- How do you handle deadlines?
- How do you handle disagreements within a team?
- Where do you see yourself in five years?

Important Note

The actual recruitment process is role-specific. Candidates should follow the
instructions in their assessment invitation and communication from the
recruiting team.

There may not be the same number of interview rounds for every Accenture
position.
