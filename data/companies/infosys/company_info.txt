INFOSYS
Company Information

1. Company Overview

Infosys Limited is a global technology services and consulting
company.

Infosys was established in 1981 by seven engineers and has grown into
a global organization serving clients across multiple countries.

The company focuses on digital transformation, consulting,
technology services, cloud, artificial intelligence, and enterprise
solutions.

2. Purpose

Infosys describes its purpose as:

"To amplify human potential and create the next opportunity for
people, businesses and communities."

3. Areas of Business

Infosys provides services and solutions in areas including:

- Consulting
- Technology services
- Digital transformation
- Cloud
- Artificial intelligence
- Data and analytics
- Application development
- Enterprise solutions
- Engineering services
- Cybersecurity
- Business process services

4. Technology Focus

Infosys works with technologies and platforms across areas such as:

Infosys operates globally and serves clients across numerous countries.
