TCS
Interview and Hiring Process

1. Application

Candidates apply through the appropriate TCS careers or hiring portal
for an available position or hiring program.

2. Profile Evaluation

TCS evaluates submitted candidate profiles against the requirements
and job description associated with the opportunity.

3. Shortlisting

Candidates who meet the applicable eligibility and role requirements
may be shortlisted for further stages.

4. Assessment

Depending on the hiring program, candidates may be required to
complete an assessment or test.

The exact assessment structure varies by hiring program.

5. Interview

Shortlisted candidates may be invited for an interview.

The interview format and number of stages can vary depending on the
specific position and hiring program.

6. Selection

Candidates who successfully complete the applicable selection stages
may proceed toward the hiring/onboarding process.

7. Important Note

There is no single interview process that applies to every TCS role.

The exact stages depend on the job, hiring program, candidate category,
and recruitment drive.

Candidates should refer to the specific TCS job posting or hiring
program for current requirements.
