TATA CONSULTANCY SERVICES (TCS)
Company Information

1. Company Overview

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is a global IT services, consulting and
business solutions organization and a part of the Tata Group.

TCS was founded in 1968 and provides technology and business services to
organizations across industries worldwide.

The company describes itself as a technology partner for industry-leading
organizations and focuses on innovation, engineering excellence,
customer service, and long-term value creation.

2. Areas of Business

TCS provides services across areas including:

- IT services
- Consulting
- Business solutions
- Technology services
- Engineering services
- Digital transformation
- Artificial intelligence
- Cloud
- Enterprise technology
- Industry-specific technology solutions

TCS serves organizations across multiple industries, including:

- Banking and financial services
- Insurance
- Retail and consumer packaged goods
- Communications and media
- Manufacturing
- Life sciences and healthcare
- Energy and utilities
- Travel and hospitality
- Technology and services

3. Technology and Innovation

TCS is investing in artificial intelligence and describes its strategic
direction around becoming an AI-powered technology services organization.

Its technology portfolio spans areas from infrastructure through
intelligence, including cloud, AI, enterprise applications, engineering,
and digital transformation.

4. Global Presence

TCS operates internationally and has a large global workforce and
delivery network serving clients across multiple geographic regions.

5. Career Environment

TCS provides career opportunities across software development,
technology consulting, engineering, AI, cloud, data, cybersecurity,
enterprise applications, and other technology areas.

The TCS careers organization provides opportunities through its India
careers portal and role-specific job postings.
