INFOSYS
Interview and Hiring Process

1. General Recruitment

Infosys uses a recruitment and selection process that can vary
depending on the role.

The process may include:

- Initial screening
- Technical or functional interviews
- Discussions related to cultural alignment and shared values

2. Power Programmers Hiring Process

For the Infosys Power Programmers / Strategic Technology Group
program, the published hiring flow includes:

Stage 1 — Registration

Candidates register through the relevant hiring initiatives,
campus connections, or referrals.

Stage 2 — Coding Rounds

Candidates undergo coding assessments designed to evaluate technical
expertise and problem-solving ability.

Stage 3 — Technical Interviews

Candidates participate in live technical coding interviews with
technologists.

The interviews can cover a wider range of technology areas.

Stage 4 — Final Selection

Candidates selected after the applicable stages may proceed to
internships, job-level training, or direct deployment on projects,
depending on the program.

3. Important Note

The Power Programmers process is a specific Infosys hiring program.

It should not be assumed that every Infosys position follows exactly
the same sequence or number of rounds.

Candidates should refer to the current job posting or hiring program
for the applicable recruitment process.
